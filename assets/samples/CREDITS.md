# Instrument samples

Per-note MP3s from the **Fluid (R3) General MIDI SoundFont** by Frank Wen, as pre-rendered by
[gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts) (`FluidR3_GM/`).

License: [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
The app credits it in Studio Settings.

| Folder | GM instrument | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `grand/` | acoustic_grand_piano | C, Eb, Gb, A from C3 to A5 (also used, lowpassed, for "Felt") |
| `rhodes/` | electric_piano_1 | C, Eb, Gb, A from C3 to A5 |
| `vibes/` | vibraphone | C, Eb, Gb, A from C4 to C6 |
| `flute/` | flute | C, Eb, Gb, A from C4 to C6 |

Unchanged from the source files. Tone's Sampler repitches the notes in between.
